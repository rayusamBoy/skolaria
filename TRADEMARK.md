The following uses are **not permitted** under any circumstances:

- Using "Skolaria" (or any confusingly similar name such as
  "Skolaria Pro", "Skoolaris", "Skolaria Cloud", "SkolarisHost")
  for a fork, product, hosted service, or commercial offering.
- Using the Skolaria logo in a way that suggests endorsement,
  partnership, or official status that we have not granted.
- Modifying the logo — changing colours, proportions, adding
  elements, or combining it with other marks.
- Registering domains, social handles, or package names on public
  registries that could be mistaken for the official project.
- Selling, sublicensing, or hosting Skolaria as a paid service
  without a written agreement with us.

## Allowed without asking

You do **not** need to contact us for any of the following:

### Personal and non-commercial use

- Cloning this repository and running it on your own server —
  whether for your school, your computer lab, or your personal
  development.
- Deploying Skolaria inside your school to manage your own
  students, staff, and finances.
- Making modifications for your own use. (If you distribute them,
  the AGPL-3.0 still applies.)

### Referencing the project

- Saying your project "uses Skolaria", is "built on Skolaria",
  or is "compatible with Skolaria".
- Linking to [https://skolaria.co.tz](https://skolaria.co.tz) or this repository from blogs,
  tutorials, videos, courses, and documentation.
- Writing about Skolaria in articles, papers, or talks.
- Using the unmodified logo to indicate that your project or
  tutorial is about Skolaria.

### Community contributions

- Submitting pull requests, issues, or documentation to this
  repository.
- Publishing tutorials, plugins, or extensions that clearly
  state they are for Skolaria (without implying they are
  official).

### Screenshots and demos

- Using screenshots of the Skolaria interface in tutorials,
  documentation, blog posts, and product comparisons.

## Requires written permission

You must have a written agreement with us to:

- Offer Skolaria (or a modified version) as a **paid hosted service**.
- Sell support, installation, or training under the Skolaria name.
- Distribute Skolaria as part of a **commercial product**.
- Use the Skolaria name or logo on **merchandise** (t-shirts,
  stickers, mugs, etc.).
- Register a domain that contains "Skolaria" for commercial use.
- Use the Skolaria mark in advertising, sponsorships, or
  co-branded materials.
- Publish an app store listing or marketplace listing under the
  Skolaria name.

If your intended use is commercial, or if you are unsure whether
it is, email **rysmtulia07@gmail.com** before proceeding. We are usually happy to grant
permission for commercial use when the request is clear and does not
create confusion with the official project.

## When we may revoke permission

Even if a use is listed above as allowed, we reserve the right to
withdraw permission if the use:

- Damages the reputation of Skolaria or its community.
- Misleads users about the origin or sponsorship of a product.

If that happens, we will contact you first and give you a reasonable
opportunity to comply.
